Score = 40% shared interests + 25% overlapping dates + 20% pace match + 15% budget fit. No AI in this path by design — every match is fully explainable.

No open trips match those dates or destination — try widening either.

Candidates include real open trips other accounts created, plus a few seed trips the server ships with so there's something to match against right away. Approving a join request is simulated instantly since there's no real trip owner reviewing it live in this demo.

After the trip

Find yourself
in every photo.

Upload your trip photos and we'll group them by who's in them — a real face-detection model runs right here in your browser, on your device. Only the resulting face thumbnails and any name you add are saved, tied to your active trip.

No faces grouped yet — turn on grouping above and upload some photos.

Faces are clustered by similarity only — no cluster is ever auto-labelled with a real name. Add a name yourself if you want to. The original uploaded photos themselves are never sent anywhere — only the cropped face thumbnails and labels are saved to this trip.
